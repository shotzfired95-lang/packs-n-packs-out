Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information Packs N’ Packs Out collects when you use our store, why we collect it, and what we do — and don’t do — with it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and mobile phone number when you create an account.
  • Order information: the address you give us for delivery or shipping, your order history, and the details of what you bought.
  • Store wallet and credit activity: your in-store balance, top-ups, welcome credit, membership status, and credit-line activity on this store.
  • Messenger link: if you choose to connect Messenger for order alerts, we store the link between your store account and that Messenger chat so alerts can reach you.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose “Continue with Google,” Google shares your basic profile — your name and email address — with us so we can create or sign in to your account. We never see your Google password.
  • Support messages: anything you send us through the store’s support tickets.

Why we collect it

  • To take, prepare, deliver, or hold your orders for pickup.
  • To run your account, membership, store wallet, and credit features.
  • To send you order updates and alerts — on the store, by push notification if you turn it on, or in Messenger if you connect it.
  • To prevent duplicate or abusive accounts (for example, one account per phone number).
  • To answer your support questions and sort out order problems.

What we don’t do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We don’t store your card numbers. Payments are handled through the payment providers listed at checkout; your card details go to them, not to us.
  • We don’t share your information with advertisers.

Cookies and local storage

Our store uses your browser’s local storage to remember your cart, keep you signed in, and save your preferences on your device. This stays on your device — clearing your browser storage clears it.

Push notifications

Push notifications are off unless you turn them on in your account. If you enable them, we use them for order and account alerts. You can turn them off any time in your account settings or your browser.

Your choices

You can update your account details in My Account at any time. If you want us to correct or delete your information, or you have any question about this policy, contact us and we’ll take care of it.
