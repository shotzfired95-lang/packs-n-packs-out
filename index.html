Good Questions

FAQ

How does order-ahead pickup work?
Add products to your cart, check out with your name and phone number, and choose a pickup time. Pay right at checkout — Apple Pay, Cash App, Chime, Current, or Bitcoin — or, if you’re an approved member, pay cash at the counter when you arrive. Either way, bring your ID. We pack your order and hold it at the counter; most orders are ready in about 30 minutes.
What do I need to buy?
A valid, government-issued photo ID showing you’re 21 or older. We check ID at pickup for every order, every time — no exceptions.
How do I pay?
Six ways to pay at checkout: Apple Pay, Cash App, Chime, Current, Bitcoin, or cash. Cash App, Chime, and Current are plain handle-to-handle — send to the business handle shown at checkout and enter your own handle so we know it’s yours. Cash is members-only: we accept cash from approved members, and an approved member’s cash order carries a Cash Acceptance Code that’s entered at pickup or delivery to complete the cash transaction. Delivery orders can be paid ahead by Apple Pay, Cash App, Chime, Current, or Bitcoin — approved members can also pay cash to the driver. Pickup orders can also be paid in cash at the counter by approved members.
Do you deliver?
Yes — local delivery is available in select areas of our Illinois market for orders that meet the $35 minimum. Pay at checkout by Apple Pay, Cash App, Chime, Current, or Bitcoin — approved members can also choose cash on delivery and pay the driver. Have your photo ID (21+) ready at the door.
I run a licensed shop — how do I buy wholesale?
Head to the Wholesale portal from the menu or footer. Licensed retailers and distributors can apply for a wholesale account with case pricing, scheduled delivery, and a dedicated rep. Your license is verified before the account opens.
What if something’s wrong with my order?
Bring it back with your receipt and we’ll make it right. Unopened products can be exchanged within the window printed on your receipt, and defective products are replaced — that’s the standard we run on.

Membership

Pick your tier — save on every order

Your member discount is your tier’s percent off your cart total — 5% for Silver, 10% for Gold, 15% for Platinum, 15% for Diamond — applied automatically to the product subtotal at checkout while your membership is active. Your membership discount doesn’t stack on already-discounted items — bundles and drops are priced as shown; your tier percent applies only to the full-price items in your cart. Bundle pricing is a member perk, too — any active membership unlocks it. One membership per account, tied to your account email. Joining — and every paid renewal while you stay subscribed — also drops welcome credit straight into your account balance the moment your payment is confirmed and your membership activates: $5 for Silver, $10 for Gold, $20 for Platinum, $35 for Diamond on the monthly term, multiplied on prepaid terms, up to $105 on a 12-month Diamond. Every join starts pending: you pick how you’re paying, and nothing — no discount, no credit, no perks — switches on until the business confirms your payment landed and you enter the activation code they give you. And every year on the anniversary of the day you first joined, anniversary credit lands in your balance while your membership stays active: $10 for Silver, $20 for Gold, $35 for Platinum, $50 for Diamond.

Pick a term — the longer the term, the bigger the cut off the membership fee, charged once up front for the whole term. The term discount is on the fee only: your tier’s percent off your orders is the same on every term. Cancelling stops renewal; benefits run to the end of the paid term and fees aren’t refunded mid-term.

Membership fees are paid up front for the term you pick, through the payment method you choose when you join — Cash App, Chime, Current, Apple Pay, Bitcoin, or cash for approved members. Card is not accepted for memberships. Perks waive delivery and shipping fees only — order minimums still apply. Joining — from this page or at checkout — starts a pending membership: your tier, your discount, and your welcome credit switch on only when the business confirms your payment landed and you enter your activation code, and your term starts that day. Until then, orders charge full price. No mid-term refunds — cancelling stops the next renewal and your benefits run to the end of the term you paid for. Welcome credit is store credit under the same wallet rules — it never expires and can’t be cashed out — with one exception: it can’t pay membership fees, balance top-ups, or credit-line tabs. Cancel and rejoin the same tier within 12 months of your last welcome grant for that tier and no new grant drops; continuous renewals always grant. Credit line and welcome credit can only be redeemed while your membership is active.
